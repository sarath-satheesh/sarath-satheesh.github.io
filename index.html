Driving simulator

Driver Perspective Simulator

TH Deggendorf · Master's case study, 2025 · team project

A driving simulator built in Unity by a university team, in the VR/AR case study course. The player drives a car through a city, and when the car passes a trigger point, a pedestrian starts to cross the road. The level ends when the car reaches the pedestrian, and the project has several levels.

My part: game logic in Unity scripts, such as collisions, spawning pedestrians and cars, level switching and on-screen text, and all the sound of the game, including car sounds, city ambience, a pedestrian warning and a collision sound.

Profile-shifted gears

Involute Gear: Effect of Profile Shift

TH Deggendorf · Additive Manufacturing case study, 2024 · team of three

A parametric involute gear generator written in IceSL (Lua), with profile shift as an input. The script builds a pair of meshing gears with fillets, shaft pins, a handle and a stand, ready for FFF 3D printing and assembly as a demonstration model. The team also wrote a technical report on the gear geometry and a user guide for the script.

Team: Bennet Kurian, Sarath Satheesh, Sajin Saji · Advisor: Prof. Dr.-Ing. Stefan Scherbarth
